AI stands for Artificial Intelligence.

* Artificial means something made by humans, not by nature.
* Intelligence means the ability to think, learn, and solve problems.

So, AI is basically computer software or machines that are taught to think and learn almost like a human brain.

Normally, a regular computer program can only do what a human tells it to do, step by step. But with AI, a computer can look at a lot of information, learn from it, and make its own smart decisions.

You probably use AI every day without even knowing it! Here are a few simple examples:

* Voice Assistants: Siri or Alexa listening to your voice and answering your questions.
* Recommendations: YouTube or Netflix suggesting a new video or movie you might like.
* Maps: GPS apps finding the fastest route to avoid traffic.

In short, AI is like giving a computer a brain so it can help humans do things faster and easier!
